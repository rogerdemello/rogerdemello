## ✨ Featured Projects

Each of these is live - click **Demo** to try it, **Code** to read it.

### 🤖 Autonomous Executive Email Copilot
A multi-agent Agentic AI framework with a deterministic, RL-style simulation environment for benchmarking autonomous email agents across classification, prioritization, and full action execution. RAG-powered prioritization cut estimated inbox-triage time by ~60%.
`Multi-Agent RAG` `LangChain` `FastAPI` `React` `SciPy`
&nbsp;&nbsp;▸ [**Live Demo**](https://exec-email-copilot.onrender.com) · [**Code**](https://github.com/rogerdemello/autonomous-executive-email-copilot)

### 📡 Shadow GTM - Autonomous Go-To-Market Intelligence
A multi-tenant SaaS platform that watches competitors, diffs live web data, and delivers ranked, source-grounded sales plays. Includes a conversational analyst agent, battlecards, Stripe billing, and a public REST API with webhooks.
`Next.js` `React 19` `Gemini API` `Supabase` `Stripe` `TypeScript`
&nbsp;&nbsp;▸ [**Live Demo**](https://shadow-gtm.vercel.app) · [**Code**](https://github.com/rogerdemello/shadow-gtm)

### 🛡️ DealSentry - AI Proposal & Compliance Guard
An enterprise AI platform that automates proposal review with AI risk scoring and DOCX/PDF ingestion - reducing manual review effort by ~70%. Ships with a rules engine, RBAC, SLA tracking, and Salesforce/HubSpot/Gmail integrations.
`Azure OpenAI` `RAG` `React` `Express` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL`
&nbsp;&nbsp;▸ [**Live Demo**](https://dealsentry.onrender.com/) · [**Code**](https://github.com/rogerdemello/DealSentry)

### ❤️ LifePulse - Health Analytics & Prediction
An ML health platform with four trained models - heart disease (**87.2%**), sleep disorder, migraine, and personalized health scoring (0.82 R²) - plus a USDA-integrated nutrition tracker, deployed with ONNX offline inference.
`Scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `ONNX` `Pandas` `Flask`
&nbsp;&nbsp;▸ [**Live Demo**](https://lifepulse-9vz4.onrender.com/) · [**Code**](https://github.com/rogerdemello/LifePulse)

### 🔮 SentinelOps - Predictive Incident Operations
An autonomous incident-prediction platform processing 10,000+ telemetry events with IsolationForest anomaly detection and dependency-graph modeling - multi-agent root-cause analysis and human-approved remediation cut estimated MTTD by ~80%.
`FastAPI` `IsolationForest` `statsmodels` `NetworkX` `React` `Supabase`
&nbsp;&nbsp;▸ [**Code**](https://github.com/rogerdemello/sentinel-ops)
